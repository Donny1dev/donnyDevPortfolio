Good questions

Things people actually ask us first.

Can't find what you need? Just write it into the enquiry form above — we read everything.

How much does a typical renovation cost? +
It depends — but honestly, that's not a dodge. A full kitchen typically lands $35–90k. A whole-of-house renovation on a Queenslander runs $250–600k. We'll give you an honest range on the first call, before you've committed a thing.
How long does the process take from first chat to keys? +
For a kitchen or single-room project, expect 8–14 weeks from first call. A full home renovation is closer to 6–10 months including design and approvals. We don't quote shorter than we can deliver — we'd rather lose the job than miss the date.
Do you handle council approvals? +
Yes. We do all the documentation, draftwork, and lodgement in-house. You'll never have to chase a council officer or interpret a planning scheme — that's our job.
What if I don't have plans or even a clear idea yet? +
Perfectly fine — about half our clients arrive that way. The first call is mostly listening. We'll ask about how you live, what frustrates you about the current place, and what you'd like more of. Plans come later.
Are you licensed and insured? +
Fully QBCC licensed, fully insured, and we work to AS standards across all trades. Documentation available on request — we'd rather you ask.
